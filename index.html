English

momongs Inc. operates Three Kingdoms: Path of Warlords, an offline single-player game. For privacy questions, contact help@momongs.com.

Data handled by the game

The current game does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, or online gameplay. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, or microphone, and it does not send gameplay or personal information to momongs Inc. or third-party services. Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

Store and support services

Google Play processes downloads, purchases, payments, and related device information under Google's Privacy Policy. Google may provide us with order and app performance records through Play Console; we do not receive your full payment card details. If you email us for support, we use your email address and the information you provide only to respond to your request. Our email service provider processes that correspondence for delivery and storage. We do not sell support information.

Retention, deletion, and security

Local game progress and settings remain on your device until you delete them or clear or uninstall the app. Support correspondence is kept only for as long as needed to handle your request or meet a legal obligation, then deleted. You may request deletion of support correspondence at help@momongs.com. We limit access to support messages to people handling the request and use the security controls of our email provider.

Changes

We will update this page if the game's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

스토어 및 고객지원

Google Play는 다운로드, 구매, 결제 및 관련 기기 정보를 Google 개인정보처리방침에 따라 처리합니다. Google은 Play Console을 통해 주문 및 앱 실적 기록을 당사에 제공할 수 있으나, 당사는 결제 카드의 전체 정보를 받지 않습니다. 고객지원 이메일을 보내면 답변을 위해 이메일 주소와 문의 내용을 사용합니다. 이메일 서비스 제공업체가 메일의 전송과 보관을 처리하며, 당사는 문의 정보를 판매하지 않습니다.
